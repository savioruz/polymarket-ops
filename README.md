# polymarket-ops

**AI-powered Polymarket copy-trading bot.** Watch top traders on [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com), mirror their positions with your own risk controls.

## Features

- 🐋 **Copy-trade top Polymarket traders** automatically
- 🤖 **AI-assisted signal interpretation** — parse trader behavior into actionable trades
- 🛡️ **Risk controls** — position caps, slippage guards, whitelisted markets
- 📈 **Portfolio tracking** — PnL, exposure, and per-trader attribution

## Tech Stack

`Go` · `AI` · `Polymarket API` · `WebSocket` · `PostgreSQL`

## Status

Early-stage. **Use at your own risk** — prediction markets are volatile and this is experimental trading software.

## Disclaimer

Not financial advice. Trading on prediction markets carries risk of loss.
